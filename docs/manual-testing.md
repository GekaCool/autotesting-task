# Part 2 - Manual Testing

## Exploratory session notes

**Charter:** "The financing / monthly payment calculator and car filtering, on a mobile viewport." (40 min, timeboxed)

**Environment:** stage.longo.lv, Chrome DevTools mobile emulation, iPhone 16 Pro Max viewport (440x956, 75% zoom), locale EN.

**What was covered**
- Financing check form (`/en/financing/form`) end-to-end on the mobile viewport: the "Your Budget", "Your income and liabilities", "Your information" and "Address" sections, focusing on field-level input validation (text, numeric, and date fields).
- Car catalog (`/en/catalog`) on the mobile viewport: opening and using the filters panel.
- General page layout and usability at the mobile breakpoint while working through the above (see [mobile-view.png](evidence/mobile-view.png)).

**What was consciously not covered**
- Completing a financing application past the "Verify identity" step - deliberately not pursued.
- The `lv` and `ru` locales - the session stayed in `en`.
- Desktop/tablet viewports - out of scope for this charter.
- Verifying the monthly payment calculation itself against an independent formula - noted as a good candidate for a follow-up session, not attempted here.

**What was found:** the six issues logged in the Bug reports table below (BUG-01 through BUG-06), all in the financing form and the catalog Year filter.
